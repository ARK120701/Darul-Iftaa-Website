Give for the Sake of Allah

Your sadaqah sustains the work of Darul Iftaa New York — our Muftis, our training program, and our free public fatwa portal.

Why Your Donation Matters

Darul Iftaa New York operates as a free, charitable service of Darul Uloom New York. We do not charge for fataawas, and every donation goes directly toward keeping this service running — and growing — for the community.

Your Impact

$25/month Covers hosting and research materials for our public fatwa portal
$500 Supports an Ifta Training Program student for a semester
$1,000+ Major donor — named endowment opportunity
