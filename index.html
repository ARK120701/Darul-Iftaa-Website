Welcome to Darul Iftaa New York

Darul Iftaa New York is the fatwā department of Darul Uloom New York, established to serve the everyday jurisprudential needs of the Muslim community — from personal worship and family matters to modern business and finance.

Every question submitted to our office is researched and reviewed by a panel of qualified Muftīs before a ruling is issued, ensuring that each answer is authentic, reliable, and grounded in the Ḥanafī school of Islamic law.
