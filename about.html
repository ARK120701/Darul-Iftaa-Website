Understanding Our Institution

What is a Darul Ifta?

A Darul Ifta is a vital center where individuals are available to answer a vast array of questions pertaining to Islam and its teachings. It functions as a significant institution or advisory body primarily responsible for providing religious rulings, which are commonly referred to as Fatāwā. These Fatāwā are meticulously issued in response to inquiries or concerns from both individuals and organizations that actively seek guidance on important aspects of Islamic law (Shariah).

In countries where Muslims constitute a minority, there arises a pronounced need for the community to comprehend how to effectively fulfill their religious obligations while remaining compliant with the legal frameworks of their respective nations. This need is particularly critical in diverse societies where cultural and legal norms may differ significantly from traditional Islamic teachings.

Darul Ifta Darul Uloom New York (DUNY)

In light of this pressing necessity, DUNY took the initiative to establish a dedicated Darul Ifta department with the grace and blessings of Allah. The principal duty of this department is to offer Islamic rulings (fiqh) that are firmly grounded in the teachings of the Qur'ān and Sunnah, addressing a wide variety of essential topics. These topics include, but are not limited to, religious practices, financial transactions, family issues, dietary concerns, social responsibilities, the rites of funerals, matters regarding wills, and inheritance.

The Fatāwā issued by Darul Ifta can be intricately tailored to reflect specific personal circumstances or can be more general in nature, aiming to cater to the diverse needs of the community. Additionally, Darul Ifta DUNY is committed to ensuring that contemporary issues faced by Muslims are addressed within the context of Islamic law. This commitment involves the intricate process of reconciling modern challenges with the enduring principles of traditional Islamic teachings, thereby ensuring that the rulings provided remain not only relevant but also practical in today’s world.

In summary, Darul Ifta serves as a vital and indispensable institution for Islamic legal thought, acting as a crucial bridge between the timeless teachings of religious law and the evolving circumstances of modern life. This role enables Muslims to fulfill their religious duties while skillfully adapting to contemporary societal norms and challenges, fostering a better understanding of their faith in a rapidly changing world.

Our Mission & Approach

Darul Iftaa New York exists because Muslims living in America face real, everyday questions — about worship, family, and finance — that deserve careful, informed answers. As the fatwa department of Darul Uloom New York, we bridge classical Islamic scholarship and contemporary life.

Every fatwa issued by our office passes through a process of multi-Mufti review: a question is researched against the primary sources — the Quran and Sunnah — and weighed within the Hanafi school of fiqh, before a written ruling is finalized and delivered.

Our Mission

To provide the Muslim community of New York with free, confidential, and authentic Islamic legal rulings, researched and reviewed by qualified Muftis.

Our Approach

Every ruling is grounded in the Quran and Sunnah and reasoned through the Hanafi school of fiqh, cross-checked by more than one Mufti before it is published.

Our Commitment

We are committed to making sound religious guidance accessible to every Muslim — individuals, families, and business owners alike — free of charge.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every ruling issued by Darul Iftaa New York.

Authenticity

Every ruling is rooted directly in the Quran, Sunnah, and the established texts of the Hanafi madhab — never mere opinion.

Accessibility

Our services are free and confidential, and we welcome questions from every walk of life — no question is too small.

Rigor

Each fatwa passes through careful research and multi-Mufti review before it is finalized and shared publicly.

Our Journey

From the founding of Darul Uloom New York to the establishment of a dedicated fatwa department.

2010

Darul Uloom New York Founded

Darul Uloom New York was founded to bring authentic Islamic learning to the heart of New York, laying the scholarly foundation for everything that followed.

2016

First Alim Course Graduates

The institute's first cohort of Alim Course graduates completed their studies, several of whom would go on to specialize in Islamic jurisprudence.

2022

Darul Iftaa Formally Established

Darul Iftaa New York was formally established as a dedicated department, staffed by trained Muftis, to serve the fatwa needs of the wider community.
