Give for the Sake of Allāh

Your ṣadaqah sustains the work of Darul Iftaa New York — our Muftīs, our training program, and our free public fatwā portal.

Why Your Donation Matters

Darul Iftaa New York operates as a free, charitable service of Darul Uloom New York. We do not charge for fatāwā, and every donation goes directly toward keeping this service running — and growing — for the community.

Your Impact
