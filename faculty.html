Our Panel of Muftis

Every ruling issued by our office is researched and reviewed by qualified Muftis specializing in different areas of fiqh.

Mufti, Darul Iftaa

Fiqh & Fatwa

Family & Marriage Law

Mufti, Darul Iftaa

Marriage & Family

Specializes in marriage, divorce, and family jurisprudence, handling sensitive matters with care and confidentiality.

Business & Finance

Mufti, Darul Iftaa

Islamic Finance

Worship & Purification

Mufti, Darul Iftaa

Handles rulings related to Salah, purification, fasting, and the day-to-day acts of worship.
