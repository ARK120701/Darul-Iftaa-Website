About Darul Iftaa New York

The fatwa department of Darul Uloom New York — dedicated to answering the Muslim community's questions with sound, well-researched rulings.

وَعَلَّمَكَ مَا لَمْ تَكُن تَعْلَمُ

Our Mission & Approach

Darul Iftaa New York exists because Muslims living in America face real, everyday questions — about worship, family, and finance — that deserve careful, informed answers. As the fatwa department of Darul Uloom New York, we bridge classical Islamic scholarship and contemporary life.

Every fatwa issued by our office passes through a process of multi-Mufti review: a question is researched against the primary sources — the Quran and Sunnah — and weighed within the Hanafi school of fiqh, before a written ruling is finalized and delivered.

Our Mission

To provide the Muslim community of New York with free, confidential, and authentic Islamic legal rulings, researched and reviewed by qualified Muftis.

Our Approach

Every ruling is grounded in the Quran and Sunnah and reasoned through the Hanafi school of fiqh, cross-checked by more than one Mufti before it is published.

Our Commitment

We are committed to making sound religious guidance accessible to every Muslim — individuals, families, and business owners alike — free of charge.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every ruling issued by Darul Iftaa New York.

Authenticity

Every ruling is rooted directly in the Quran, Sunnah, and the established texts of the Hanafi madhab — never mere opinion.

Accessibility

Our services are free and confidential, and we welcome questions from every walk of life — no question is too small.

Rigor

Each fatwa passes through careful research and multi-Mufti review before it is finalized and shared publicly.

Our Journey

From the founding of Darul Uloom New York to the establishment of a dedicated fatwa department.

2010

Darul Uloom New York Founded

Darul Uloom New York was founded to bring authentic Islamic learning to the heart of New York, laying the scholarly foundation for everything that followed.

2016

First Alim Course Graduates

The institute's first cohort of Alim Course graduates completed their studies, several of whom would go on to specialize in Islamic jurisprudence.

2022

Darul Iftaa Formally Established

Darul Iftaa New York was formally established as a dedicated department, staffed by trained Muftis, to serve the fatwa needs of the wider community.
