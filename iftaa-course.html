Takhassus fil-Ifta — Iftaa Course

Advanced post-graduate specialization in the science of issuing Fatawa

Alim Graduates Only Multi-Year Program

Takhassus fil-Ifta, commonly known as the Iftaa Course, is a rigorous multi-year specialization for graduates of the Alim program who wish to master the methodology of issuing Islamic legal rulings. Students train directly under the senior Muftis of Darul Iftaa New York, moving from theoretical study into supervised, real-world fatwa research.

The program is one of the most demanding offered by Darul Uloom New York. Graduates emerge equipped to serve as Muftis themselves — researching questions from the primary sources, weighing juristic opinions, and communicating rulings clearly to the people who ask them.

Eligibility

Admission is open exclusively to graduates of the Alim Course (or an equivalent recognized Dars-e-Nizami program), who are then evaluated through interview and assessment before acceptance.

What Students Study

Usul al-Fiqh (principles of Islamic jurisprudence)
Comparative Fiqh across the four Madhabs
Fatwa methodology and research technique
Contemporary applied fiqh (finance, medicine, technology)
Direct mentorship by senior Muftis
Supervised drafting of real fatwa responses
